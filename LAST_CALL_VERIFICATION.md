# The Last Call / native Windows verification

## What is being tested

The acceptance target is an understandable, worthwhile investigation: two sourced plans, a physically believable bay, a dated later discovery, an actual sound distraction, reliable partial saves and a coherent return to both original ending choices. Automated actions can establish geometry, state, audible-source positioning and recovery. Human understanding, fear, fun and first-play duration remain pending.

## Actual route and preserved failures

LastCall_06 drove a fresh new case from the gate through the original investigation, records store, service exit, 1998 reel, valves, death/retry, A/B comparison and copied bedroom. Its original investigation was genuinely earned. The harness then closed a bay door the Surveyor had already opened, so entry failed. It also checked UV availability before the 1.5-second battery exchange completed. This run ended 361/18 and is NOT claimed as a passing full route. Its unmodified earned landing checkpoint is preserved.

LastCall_10 resumed that checkpoint. It exposed a genuine new lure defect: after warning grace, an audible switch click did not notify hearing, so the actor could reach Eli before later taps. Hearing is now tied to the actual switch-click player as well as taps, at their real upstairs position. The same rule cannot redirect a sighted pursuit.

LastCall_11 resumed the earned landing after that correction and passed 63/0 through the physical bay experiment, interruption/reload, real warned search, source lure, maintenance escape, return to the original porch register and both earned departures, including signed-departure pause/load and completed-save menus. This is a segmented earned integration, NOT a claim that a single fresh final-binary run passed uninterrupted. No inventory, clue or ending flags were awarded to make the continuation pass.

The final LastCall_14 binary clears stale document-button handles, releases old card focus and persists the search investigation point. LastCall_12 exposed a cold-load defect: a fresh process could inherit a previous/default AI destination instead of searching the bay. LastCall_14 restores the actual saved target (with a bay fallback for earlier candidate saves). It passes 8/0 real cold-load/pursuit/death/retry/story-mode checks and 48/0 saved-search continuation through both endings, including saving again after the deck distraction and retaining that last heard source. Progression and the corrected audio source remain unchanged from LastCall_11.

## Earlier native layout crashes

Two LastCall_10 layout processes exited with Windows access violations. Event records identify UNLISTED.exe/unknown rather than establishing an NVIDIA-driver cause. A stale-button-reference defect was found in card replacement and fixed; layout transitions also allow a normal frame between views. The earlier crashes and their logs remain preserved. Clean subsequent tests do not conclusively establish the original native crash cause or prove all historical engine/shutdown/driver issues fixed. Repeated final layout tests are required before packaging.

## Focused evidence and limits

LastCall_04: actual mouse dragging/rotation with generous snap, partial placement load, real standing/collision/door traversal, removed bridge, interrupted test rewind, paused search, fresh warning on load and maintenance escape. The player physically enters the bay; it has floors, ceiling, side/back walls and a fitted door, rather than a teleport or open rectangular void.

Final tests cover manual and controller sheet control, both register eligibility layouts, clearly dated page, 1080p/1440p/3440x1440, visible-pursuit rejection, actual death/retry, nonlethal story-mode contact, and a real completed legacy save. A derived old-case porch fixture checks the old earned choices without the new case gate; it is labelled as a fixture.

Captured video uses elapsed-time native viewport samples and simultaneous game-bus audio, with repeated frames rather than speed-up or interpolation. Signal measurements establish non-silent output, not subjective sound quality or hardware playback. Screenshots are unretouched. Owner checkpoint/settings hashes are checked independently of isolated QA saves.

## Still unfinished

The larger second act and 30-60 minute human first-play target, Surveyor art redesign, final vocals, subjective mixing and broader room-art review remain unfinished. Human story comprehension, puzzle clarity, artistic approval, fear, controller hardware and pacing are not certified by these automated tests.
