## Evidence separation

- Home_01 is a preserved earlier export: two window-view assertions failed after the cabinet moved. Source QA now walks to the actual window before checking the sighting. Do not count those failed assertions as a passing final build.
- Underfloor_01 is excluded from acceptance: a lazy-loaded script had a type-inference parse error and geometry did not load. Import/export success alone did not establish runtime success; the native scenario exposed it.
- Underfloor_02/03 are development captures. Underfloor_03 completed a fresh route plus earned alternate in 342.54 seconds (215 checks). It precedes the final rack-phase, source-selection and contact corrections.
- Underfloor_06 is the full-route/regression candidate with final collision/save/AI/input/story/audio behavior. Underfloor_07 only seats non-colliding files on measured shelf surfaces and applies cardboard material. The code delta and ordinary-runtime fingerprints are preserved with the delivery.
- Final maintenance checks derive support from the imported render mesh's upward-facing vertices, alongside actual screenshots, rather than assuming a guessed prop height is correct. Major-room views are labelled powered visual fixtures at player eye height, not traversal or human quality claims.
- The full fresh route and alternate from its naturally earned porch checkpoint are one shared investigation plus two earned branches, not two independent fresh playthroughs. Original timestamps, sampled frames and game-bus audio are preserved. Capture cadence is not an FPS benchmark. Exact duration and counts are in BUILD_MANIFEST.json.

All final reports must pass before the packaging tool seals the milestone. The manifest identifies final and prior-build tests explicitly. Live save/settings hashes are checked; QA uses isolated files/tagged input, with no desktop key injection and no restoration over newer human data.
