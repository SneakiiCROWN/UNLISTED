## Preservation and limits

New Game opts into case_v3. Older schema-1 saves without that event retain earlier completion requirements. All QA uses isolated checkpoint/settings files and tagged input; no desktop-input injection. Live player data is hash checked and never restored over later human changes. Field Return 0.5.6 remains the rollback release.

This is a short alpha with one added investigation chapter. The 30-60-minute target is not achieved or human measured. Surveyor art, further substantial exploration, environmental detail and audio variety remain production work. Native captures and non-silent game-bus audio are evidence of execution/signal, not subjective listening, frightening character art or human narrative approval. Custom vocal slots remain replaceable.
