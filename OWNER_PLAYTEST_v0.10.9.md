## Controls

| Action | Keyboard / mouse | Xbox-style controller |
|---|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD / mouse | Left / right sticks |
| Interact, pick up or release | E | X |
| Crouch | Hold Ctrl | B toggle, or hold LB |
| Sprint | Shift | Left-stick click |
| Cycle torch modes | F | RB |
| Check battery | B | D-pad Up |
| Replace battery | T | Y |
| Tap a loose torch contact | V | D-pad Down |
| Journal / optional hint | J / H | Back / D-pad Left |
| Pause | Escape | Start |
| Throw carried object | Left mouse | RT |
| Rotate carried object | R + mouse | LT + right stick |
| Menu accept / back | Enter / Escape | A / B |

Sprint can be set to hold or toggle in Settings. B crouches during gameplay and goes back while a menu or document is open. The settings screen lists the current control hints too.

## Before you start

Adjust mouse/stick sensitivity, volume and visibility to your screen. Retro strength is adjustable; documents stay sharp. Captions, subtitles, reduced flicker, reduced motion and Story Mode are available. Story Mode preserves the investigation while removing lethal captures. Existing settings are retained, so your previous choices take precedence over new-game defaults.

## Explore naturally

Look at objects before expecting an interaction prompt. Read physical clues and listen to where sounds come from. Your journal keeps discoveries; hints are optional. A quiet room is not necessarily a missed trigger. You can crouch, break sight and use real cover; sprinting is limited by stamina. The torch has spare batteries and a backup, so running low should not make the investigation impossible.

## If something goes wrong

Note where you were, what you touched, what you expected, and what actually happened. A screenshot or short clip helps. Mention keyboard/controller, screen resolution, Story Mode and reduced-motion settings. If the game closes, keep the build and note the approximate time; do not change drivers or delete saves to troubleshoot it.

If possible, describe confusing moments in your own words before looking at the separate verification report. Understanding the story, tension, pacing and artistic quality still need your human assessment. This guide intentionally leaves puzzle solutions and endings out.
