This is a substantial equipment and investigation milestone, not the completed expansion or a claim of 30-60 minutes of human play.

## New playable systems

- Find a fitted inspection head in the service shed. The same torch cycles off, white and UV. Battery charge, packs, light mode and collected supplies persist. Check charge briefly rather than through a permanent meter. Emergency white light and a hand-wound backup prevent battery exhaustion from blocking progress.
- Examine Simon's report sleeve using a local UV sweep. Normal light does not show the ink; moving the light matters. Only scanned lines enter the journal. An assisted slow scan and controller inspection actions are available. The world seal also responds to beam direction, distance and occlusion.
- New games save their own radio station and service-line assignments. Read actual circuit names rather than memorising colours. Older saves keep their existing answers and completed investigations.
- A fitted office cupboard provides real physical cover during a new post-inspection search. Quiet unseen entry can work; being observed or making a nearby impact can expose the hiding place. Doors, crouch clearance, warning, death/retry and checkpoint recovery use the existing systems.

## Controls and compatibility

F / RB cycles the light. B / D-pad Up briefly checks charge. T / Y changes a pack; with no spares and low charge, hold it to wind the backup. In paper inspection, hold and sweep with the mouse, use the right-stick inspection actions, or choose Slow automatic scan. These are new action bindings, not a claim of complete hardware-tested gamepad support throughout the game.

Extract into a new folder. Keep older builds and %APPDATA%/UNLISTED. Do not copy the incomplete public source over the complete licensed local project. New encounters and generated solutions belong to new cases; existing saves retain the legacy investigation and eligibility. Copy your profile before switching between versions: older releases do not understand the new generated solutions. The release checkpoint also preserves the pre-release owner profile locally.
