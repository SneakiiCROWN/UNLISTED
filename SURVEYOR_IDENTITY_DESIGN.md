# The Surveyor Identity / 0.10.6-dev / 26 October 2026

Private concept paintings accompany this checkpoint. They are AI-generated design
exploration, not native screenshots, licensed base models, rigged characters or
proof that the illustrated clothing can be built cleanly. Full prompts and the
two original images are preserved privately with the delivery.

| Impossible Figure | Records and observed space fail to agree; narrow blade-like body and subtly contradictory orientation | Still torso, delayed shoulder rotation | Painted anatomy is not a rig solution; face reads youthful/fashion-oriented, insufficient workwear identity. |

Observer is the provisional **silhouette** preference: a readable head/shoulder
line with relatively limited additional equipment and plausible existing rig
adaptation. This is a development recommendation, not owner artistic approval.
The follow-up face/workwear sheet still reads as an ordinary aged worker. It has
not earned a 3D construction pass. No more mannequin/coat variants were built.

The blocker is original character identity and convincing authored face/garment
art, not AI mechanics or missing animation tests. A capable character artist
could develop the Observer's lowered posture and horizontal upper silhouette,
then validate neutral construction before weights, gait or attack studies.
Neither concept painting should be used as an automatic texture projection.

The active Surveyor GLB, skeleton, animations, movement, hiding, detection and
attack code are preserved. No design is approved, rigged or promoted. Existing
failed Blender studies and their original sources remain privately preserved.

## Canon boundary

These directions suggest a function, not an identity. The Surveyor is not
established as Simon, an inspector who visited in 2018, or a new named character.
Eli's observations, Simon's 1998 records, tonight's experiment and the unreliable
2018 equipment label remain distinct. Both ending meanings are unchanged.
