## Performance and limitations

An explicit foreground-only QA mode displays the isolated native render viewport
in a 1280x720 desktop window. Physical PC input remains rejected; there are no
keyboard hooks or background activity monitoring. It requests foreground once;
unfocused samples are excluded. Tests render 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 3440x1440,
but do not claim an ultrawide monitor was used. Native desktop displays reported
1920x1080 and 2560x1440. Hardware: i9-14900K, RTX 4080 SUPER, Vulkan Forward+,
driver 32.0.16.2101. Per-view warmup 1.5s, sampling 3.5s, 60fps cap, no image
readback during measurement. Raw samples and final results accompany the package.
This limited stationary test is not a complete gameplay or minimum-spec benchmark.
The scene still has a high shadow/draw-call budget; sustained/lower-spec profiling
remains outstanding. No speculative global lighting downgrade was applied.

The three rooms have targeted finish improvements; they are not claimed to be
finished professional environment art. Surveyor character-art quality is still
unresolved. The copied bedroom, impossible room, existing story and both ending
meanings are preserved. Human artistic approval, fear, comprehension, subjective
audio review and first-playthrough duration remain pending. No 30-60 minute claim.
