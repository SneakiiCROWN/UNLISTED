# UNLISTED

*No Record. No Exit.*

Eli Ward takes a night call at 17 Briar Lane, the address on his father Simon's last work slip from 1998. The house has kept records of things nobody witnessed.

**Windows alpha: 0.9.0 / Inspection.** The full game and 30-60-minute target remain unfinished.

## Play

[Download the Windows ZIP](https://github.com/SneakiiCROWN/UNLISTED/releases/download/unlisted-v0.9.0-inspection-public-test-20261023/UNLISTED-Windows-Inspection-Test.zip), extract into a new folder, then open **UNLISTED.exe**. [Release, screenshots and gameplay](https://github.com/SneakiiCROWN/UNLISTED/releases/tag/unlisted-v0.9.0-inspection-public-test-20261023).

New Game starts at the front gate. Knock once, then press again to open the house door fully. Find the inspection head in the shed, investigate the actual clues, and use physical cover when threatened.

Keep older builds and copy `%APPDATA%/UNLISTED` before switching versions. Older saves migrate forward with their original answers; older executables do not understand newly generated solutions. [0.8.1 The Spare Place](https://github.com/SneakiiCROWN/UNLISTED/releases/tag/unlisted-v0.8.1-spare-place-public-test-20261022) and all earlier releases remain for rollback.
