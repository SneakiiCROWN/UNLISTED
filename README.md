# UNLISTED

*No Record. No Exit.*

Eli Ward takes a night call at 17 Briar Lane, the address on his father Simon's last work slip from 1998. The house has kept records of things nobody witnessed.

## The Last Call

- Place and turn Simon's measured tracing over an issued 1998 plan. Match recognisable stairs, walls and service lines with forgiving snap; keyboard, mouse and controller work, with optional gradual assistance.
- Find the omitted cellar doorway and inspect a properly backed maintenance recess. Disconnect the physical signal bridge and run the upstairs recording from its separate remote. The local monitor should be dead.
- Recover a dated later page and physical evidence that change Eli's understanding of why his father stayed. Simon remains missing and the Surveyor remains unidentified.
- Deliberately draw a searching Surveyor towards the real deck. Playing it while he can see Eli will not cancel pursuit. Saving preserves partial plan placement, hardware, discoveries and the search target.
- Return to the existing porch register with better context for both earned departures.

The rain/property, parked arrival car, Modern PSX settings, physical battery exchange, meaningful white/UV battery budget, controller support, service exit, impossible room, copied bedroom, underfloor rack, saved puzzles and accessibility remain.

F / RB changes light; T / Y fits a pack, or hold with no spares at low charge to wind the backup; V / D-pad Down taps a loose contact. Put carried objects down before servicing the torch. For plans, use mouse drag/scroll or arrows; controller A places, right stick moves, D-pad selects controls and B puts down.
