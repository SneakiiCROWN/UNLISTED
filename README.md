# UNLISTED

Eli Ward answers a night call at 17 Briar Lane, the address on his father Simon's last work slip from 1998.

**Current Windows prerelease: 0.7.0 Under the Floor.** Still a short alpha, not a completed 30-60-minute game.

## Play

1. [Download the Windows ZIP](https://github.com/SneakiiCROWN/UNLISTED/releases/download/unlisted-v0.7.0-under-the-floor-public-test-20261022/UNLISTED-Windows-Under-the-Floor-Test.zip).
2. Extract into a new folder and open **UNLISTED.exe**.
3. Older saves keep their previous completion conditions. Start New Game for the complete expanded investigation.

GitHub's Code / Download ZIP is incomplete public source, not the playable game. Saves/settings remain in `%APPDATA%/UNLISTED`; do not delete them when updating. [0.6.0 The Missing Minute](https://github.com/SneakiiCROWN/UNLISTED/releases/tag/unlisted-v0.6.0-missing-minute-public-test-20261021) and all earlier releases remain available for rollback.

## Under the Floor

A short physical crawl route beside the original cellar ramp leads to historical storage beneath the office. Move its rail shelving, recover Simon's test sheet, try the sound experiment and use the low opening to escape pursuit. The existing bedroom memory now has fitted bedding, worn materials, a work corner and more coherent personal detail. Pencil rubbing uses paper, handwriting and local graphite coverage, with saved partial progress and a clearly named accessibility alternative. Writing and document presentation have been revised.

The rain/exterior, impossible room, existing puzzles and both ending paths remain. No inspection gallery or rejected Surveyor art is added. Surveyor replacement character art remains unfinished. Further meaningful chapters, environmental detail, sound variety and human horror/story/art/audio assessment are still needed. See [release notes](UNDERFLOOR_RELEASE_NOTES.md), [verification](UNDERFLOOR_VERIFICATION.md) and [limitations](KNOWN_LIMITATIONS.md).

## Evidence and saves

Exported Windows tests cover actual movement, crouch/standing recovery, rack obstruction and saved motion, live pursuit into cover, physical interactions, mouse rubbing, legacy saves, death/retry, impossible room and common resolutions. One full fresh investigation and its naturally earned alternate ending are recorded, not two independent fresh runs. The final delivery changes only non-colliding decorative shelf contacts/material after that route; final maintenance/cover/mesh-support and layout checks are separate. Exact scope, hashes and automated duration are in VERIFIED_CHECKPOINT.json.

The automated known route is not first-playthrough duration. The 30-60-minute target is not claimed achieved, and passing tests is not human horror or artistic approval. Temporary owner vocal slots remain replaceable.
