## Next playable chapter / The Missing Minute

New cases add a chapter after the original report, before the porch recount:

1. **Recover an impression.** Take the original to the office's existing writing surface. Graphite rubbing reveals the torn page's indentation through player input, with an accessible complete-rubbing alternative. No timer or repeated collection task.
2. **Find the return.** The impression contains a receiver frequency and a personal detail. Tune a physical licensed radio with audible signal feedback; success returns a sound from the lounge, not an explanatory HUD instruction. Keep vocals optional and replaceable.
3. **The room that should not be here.** Only while its door is shut, the room is empty and exterior windows are unobserved, the lounge becomes a carefully furnished bedroom memory. This is separate from the preserved upside-down event. Existing shell, windows, doorway and gravity stay coherent.
4. **Read what Simon refused to sign.** The missing page gives his decision and a personal message. It makes the final alternatives about what Eli does with evidence, not an arbitrary name. The bedroom's storage has actual opening parts and believable collision.
5. **Leave the memory.** A restrained spatial encounter follows the discovery. Closing the empty room safely returns the original lounge. Save/load and pause restore the current room state without placing the player into solid furniture. Then the existing recount and both endings remain available.

The house repeats things brought to it: footsteps, a child's drawing, a familiar room. This is not proof that Eli lived at Briar Lane or that a recording is Simon's voice. Keep the Surveyor's identity uncertain; show the same obstructive presence Simon described rather than inventing a full explanation.
