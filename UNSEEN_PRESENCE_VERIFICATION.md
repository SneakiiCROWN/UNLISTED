Private development milestone. Public playable remains 0.10.0. Preserves Living

## Implemented

The established upside-down lounge remains a real furnishing/collision inversion,
not a camera rotation. Its change guard now samples 27 points across the room's
volume, including high/low corners, rather than three furniture centres. Door
closure, player/actor exclusion and out-of-sight rules remain. The copied bedroom
and recount cannot surrender room ownership to this anomaly. Narrative recognition
still requires visible furniture; a glimpse of floor does not reveal the answer.

Activation, witnessed discovery and safe restoration now checkpoint immediately.
Once Eli enters, one restrained wood contact can sound from the actual inverted
table overhead. It has an optional caption, no forced camera or explanatory popup,
and is consumed before playback. Pause freezes its delay; load preserves the spent
event. No new mandatory puzzle, identity claim, visitor or ending condition.

The Surveyor's visual attention now shares the existing AI's sight result. Warning
and pursuit do not follow the hidden player's live position after line of sight
breaks. Search/investigation retain the last known point, authored overrides retain
priority, and close capture keeps its existing eye contact. There are no additional
gaze raycasts, AI speed/damage/perception changes or new enemy vocals.

## Character art: rejected, deliberately not integrated

Reviewed the preserved Quaternius base-character licence and MakeHuman anatomy and
workwear. Produced one isolated, unrigged native-proxy workwear/inspection-hood
study without the old coat extrusion or cross-rig weight transfer. Neutral front,
side and rear renders expose body intersections, unfinished feet and an unconvincing
hood. The Windows comparison confirms it is inferior. The active model is retained.
No animation/rig acceptance is claimed for this failed static candidate. Fixing a
rig would not solve its construction or visual design; meaningful character art
still needs matched body/garment fitting and a convincing original head/footwear.
Packed Blender source and integrated game assets remain in the complete private
backup; rejected meshes are not normal game resources or public source uploads.

Corrected a demonstrated external-art QA defect: the glTF import had been called
inside an assertion, which release exports strip. Import now executes explicitly
and the test requires actual meshes. Early empty and wrongly framed comparison
captures remain preserved; only corrected 05 images are the art comparison.

## Performance

Foreground, input-isolated native-resolution views displayed in a 1280x720 desktop
window; actual movement through rainy approach, inverted furniture and real-AI
pursuit. Nine three-second samples across 1080p, 1440p and ultrawide render sizes,
60fps cap, warmed views, no image readback while sampling. Fixtures use deliberate
placements and forced pursuit in story mode. i9-14900K / RTX 4080 SUPER / Forward+.
This is broader than the prior stationary samples but is still short high-end
fixture evidence, not sustained whole-game/minimum-spec performance or a native
ultrawide-display test. Raw frame times, draw calls, movement and focus exclusions
are preserved. High draw-call/shadow pressure remains a future optimisation task.
Final 06: 1,626 focused frames, zero excluded. Nine sample medians 16.650-16.672ms,
p95 16.811-17.313ms, maximum 27.400ms. Earlier 05's ultrawide pursuit p95 reached
19.488ms; that variability remains in its receipt, rather than being hidden by the
later result. No minimum hardware or sustained 60fps guarantee follows from either.
