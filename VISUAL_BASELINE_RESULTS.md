## Initial direct visual observations

- Porch at 1080p: sign and register separated from the door, readable warm
  entrance and exposed timber/brick. Strong ceiling-lamp highlight and dark
  facade are a useful controlled light/contact comparison. Rain is visible at
  open edges. This camera looks toward the door, not at distant ground wetness.
- Lounge at 1440p: cool wall/window and warm task light produce readable room
  separation; furniture is distinguishable. Glass has little obvious reflected
  room detail in this view. Preserve the exterior sightline rather than obscuring
  it with a reflection layer. Lamp/sofa silhouettes need comparison in motion.
- Cellar ultrawide: service-panel/valve grouping reads against the beams and
  concrete; centre floor is broad and side shelving darker. Evaluate contact
  depth and material response before adding clutter or globally raising light.
