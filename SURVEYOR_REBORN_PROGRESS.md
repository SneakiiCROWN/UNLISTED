# Surveyor Reborn / isolated production work / 24 October

## Status: working study, NOT visually approved

Ordinary gameplay still loads `surveyor_presence.glb`. Neither the rejected Erasure study nor this new candidate replaces it. The new selector requires an existing isolated QA mode plus `--surveyor-reborn`. No new public playable release is authorised.

The implemented direction is an unidentified inspection worker: aged human head, continuous oilskin coat with storm collar, fitted peaked cap, work trousers and practical gloves/boots. It is not Simon. The original rig and seven retargeted clips remain; AI, collision, speeds, warning/contact timing, save schema and endings were not redesigned.

## Construction actually completed

- Inspected current actor, gaze, threat, capture, sightings and horror director; reviewed actual previous Windows baseline and rejected Erasure captures.
- Used preserved CC0 MakeHuman anatomy, workwear, skin and eye sources rather than rebuilding the old Quaternius face. Shared barycentric/rest-pose fitting, nearest-surface four-influence transfer, connected torso/sleeve garment extrusion, bound thickness, a real neck edge and packed editable Blender source were implemented.
- Preserved original finger/foot topology and weights where cross-topology transfer proved inappropriate. Extracted trouser sleeves and collar/waist fitting produced visible defects in native inspection. Retained all intermediate sources/captures and Blender crash evidence rather than treating successful export as art approval.
- Built matched baseline/candidate front, close, side, rear, torch/shadow and clip-pose fixtures through the actual Windows renderer. Added an explicit per-component diagnostic mode; hiding components is diagnostic only, not a beauty or gameplay capture.
- Added candidate-only eased animation playback rate from measured AI travel, slower idle and restrained head roll/upper-spine follow. Searching attention uses the heard/last-seen location rather than a hidden player's live position. No perception or navigation rule changes.

## What the images prove and what they do not

The model imports and preserves the skeleton/clip references. It is **not yet a convincing final antagonist**. Native images expose unresolved garment/neck fit, eye alignment after head adaptation, trouser/coat separation and extremity silhouette problems. The peaked cap and overall silhouette remain an unapproved design. Do not describe this as professionally finished, frightening to a human or ready for promotion. No rejected candidate is inserted into ordinary gameplay.

Builds 03/04/05/06 retain distinct construction/capture states. Construction subdirectories are technical correction snapshots of this direction, not owner-approved variants. Blender's Construction03 access violation and Construction04 relative texture-path failure are preserved. The builder caches plain source skin weights to avoid fragile Blender RNA iteration and resolves output paths before loading extracted eye textures.
