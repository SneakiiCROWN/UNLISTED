# UNLISTED 0.5.2 — Room Memory (public test)

A new optional environmental memory connects Eli's childhood drawing to the house.
Explore familiar rooms again after investigating the property. The event is shown
through furniture, lighting and positional sound, without an explanatory pop-up.

- The changed room saves and restores correctly, reverts safely out of sight and
  cannot visibly transform through its exterior window.
- Surveyor routes use the clear aisles; contact, death and checkpoint retry work in
  the changed room without reducing its collision or warning time.
- Eli's drawing is visible beside the TV; the loose power lead is a physical clue.
- Checkpoint recovery clears stale opening fades, captions and camera zoom.
- Carry prompts now agree with actual pickup reach.
- Existing rain, property, first-person hands, puzzles and normal saves are retained.

Final exported verification: room 44, story 34, systems 33; development companion 21.
Player-height screenshots reviewed at 1920×1080, 2560×1440 and 3440×1440. The core
full route (114), threat (54) and hands (15) passed before the final paper/wording/photo
adjustment; they are reused core evidence. Automated input was isolated from desktop
controls. Capture audio is non-silent. Human playtesting and subjective sound review
remain pending.

This is an early playable alpha. The Surveyor's character art still needs substantial
work, and broader story pacing is unfinished. One completed ending exists, with a
recoverable wrong attendance choice. No new ending or finished hour-long game is
claimed. Final creature vocals remain replaceable.

Extract into a new folder and launch UNLISTED.exe. Keep old builds and your existing
save/settings folder. The public source intentionally omits licensed asset media.
