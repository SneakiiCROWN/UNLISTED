# UNLISTED 0.5.6 / Field Return

This milestone addresses the short, unclear close-out and the practical use of objects. It is an alpha continuation, not a finished long-form game or a new Surveyor art approval.

## Player-visible changes

- The arrival and work order explain Eli's personal reason for accepting this address: it is on his missing father's last work slip. The archive connects the unidentified man to the 1998 call without claiming his identity is known.
- Simon's original field report is physical evidence in the service cabinet. On new cases, isolating the plant makes a mechanical floor release safe to use. A weighted object holds it while the player reaches the cabinet. The player can depress the plate too; walking away releases it. A mug is too light. The shed iron fitting, a tool tin and the cooking pot provide alternatives.
- The archive comparison remains optional. Its source is tonight's footsteps, not a newly invented recording of Simon speaking. It provides the evidence for the second ending.
- New-case choices explicitly distinguish correcting Simon's entry and closing the service call from attaching evidence and keeping his disappearance on record. The arbitrary Eli-name choice is removed from new cases. Signing gives back control; leaving through the property gate completes the ending. The recovered original is removed from its physical holder and retained in the journal.
- Crouching reduces the collision capsule and cannot stand through overhead solids. A maintenance screen provides real sight-blocking cover. Quiet movement and the existing AI, doors, hearing and escape still matter. A restrained edge vignette accompanies crouching near cover; it does not grant invisibility.
- The utility workbench and fuse assembly are moved away from the service-door route. Additional existing licensed kitchen/work objects can be carried, dropped and thrown. Fast turns no longer drop a held item merely because the desired grip changes sides.
- The temporary Surveyor vocal is quieter and much less frequent. Final vocal recordings remain replaceable. Its model has not been redesigned in this milestone.
- The impossible-room event retains closed-door, unoccupied and unseen safeguards. Its delay varies per case and persists with the case seed.

## Compatibility and evidence

The new investigation/departure requirement is identified by an optional event on New Game. Existing checkpoints without that event keep their original completion route. Save schema remains 1; previous builds and recovery tags remain preserved. Tests use a private input viewport and separate checkpoint files.

Final acceptance results and delivery hashes are recorded in the build manifest after native testing. Test fixtures, full routes and human assessment are different kinds of evidence. No human retest or subjective horror/audio approval is claimed.

## Still unfinished

The game remains short. A new investigation step and clearer motives do not substitute for several substantial authored chapters. The Surveyor still needs professional character-art work; rejected studies remain inactive. Broader furniture/contact audits, room detail, environmental sound variety and a richer climax remain production work. The pre-existing shutdown-resource warning and earlier NVIDIA driver crash cause are not claimed fixed.
